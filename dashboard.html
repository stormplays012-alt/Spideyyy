🎫 Ticket Studio

Build ticket panels in separate, collapsible sections. Turn features on only when you need them.

Discord-safe limits: up to 25 buttons total (5 per row × 5 rows), each label up to 80 characters, and up to 5 form questions. The dashboard checks these before posting.

1. Default ticket settings

Used as the fallback for new ticket panels.

2. Where to publish

Choose the channel where members will see your panel.

3. Panel appearance — embed, branding and description

4. Ticket buttons

Add a button visually. Every button can have its own colour, emoji, first ticket message, and form toggle.

Up to 25 buttons per message. Discord shows five buttons per row. Use a distinct button for each type of help you offer.
5. Opening form — optional, turn it off for instant ticket creation
Each button has its own form settings inside its button card: switch questions on or off, choose a form title, and write up to five questions. You can give every button a different form.
Your panel settings are saved when the panel is posted.
